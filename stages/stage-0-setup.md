**Effort:** around 1 hour
**Goal:** get access sorted, see what the exam looks like, and find out what you already know.

---

## Why this stage exists

Two reasons. First, access problems (wrong email on Google Skills, no access to the program Drive) waste days if you find them late. Sort them now. Second, taking the sample questions **before** you study feels uncomfortable but it is the single most useful thing you can do. It shows you the question style and tells you which sections need the most time.

A low baseline score is not a bad sign. It is the point.

## What the questions look like

Scenario first, then four options, and usually two of them look reasonable. Here is the shape:

> A travel company wants an AI feature that books flights, suggests hotels and answers follow-up questions, all without a person stepping in for every request. Which capability are they describing?
>
> A. A large language model
> B. An AI agent
> C. A pre-trained API
> D. A data warehouse

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B. An AI agent.** A model on its own only generates output. An agent adds a reasoning loop and the ability to call tools, which is what lets it take action across several steps.

</details>

Notice what the question did: it described **behaviour**, not a product. Most questions in this exam work that way. Learn what each thing does and the product name becomes easy.

## Set your pace

The exam takes around 20 hours of study. You do not need dates, you need a rhythm. Pick one:

| Rhythm | Sessions per week | Session length | Stages per week |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relaxed | 3 | 1 hour | 1 |
| Steady | 4 | 1.5 hours | 1 to 2 |
| Fast | 5 | 2 hours | 2 |

Write your chosen rhythm at the top of [PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md) and protect those slots.
